col-md-9"><h2>South Pole LOLA DEM Mosaic</h2><h4></h4><span style="margin-top: 2px">Michael Kenneth Barker</span><br><p><br>This product was constructed from 97 individual 20x20 km fields with 2 km overlaps.
Each field was processed with the same method as the individual regions previously created see <a href="https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/78">here</a>) and as described in Barker et al. (2021).
The fields were then individually aligned to the original DEM with a rigid 3-D translation
and blended together with a cosine taper weight in the overlap regions.
The counts (ldec) and slope (ldsm) maps of the individual fields were blended in the same way.
Hence, non-integer counts exist in the overlap regions of the final mosaicked product (solid white lines in figure).
<br>
Due to the track adjustment, cleaning, and tile blending procedures, this 87-90 deg mosaic product has a typical RMS residual of 30-55 cm with the individual fields (from <a href="https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/78">here</a>).

<h2>Data</h2>
<br>
<br>5 m/pix GeoTiffs with south polar stereographic X/Y coords in meters, MOON_ME reference frame of ephemeris DE421.
<br>
<br>X/Y coords follow the Generic Mapping Tools 'pixel-registered' convention (i.e., they are the centers of the pixels).
<br>
<br><b>ldem_87s_5mpp</b>: 3.3GB <a href="https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/LOLA_5mpp/87S/ldem_87s_5mpp.tif">GeoTIFF</a> of surface height Z (meters) with interpolation fillin
g empty pix
<br><b>ldem_87s_5mpp_hillshade</b>: 820MB <a href="https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/LOLA_5mpp/87S/ldem_87s_5mpp_hillshade.tif">GeoTIFF</a> hillshade of ldem
<br><b>ldsm_87s_5mpp</b>: 4.9GB <a href="https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/LOLA_5mpp/87S/ldsm_87s_5mpp.tif">GeoTIFF</a> of surface slope (degrees)
<br><b>ldec_87s_5mpp</b>: 140MB <a href="https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/LOLA_5mpp/87S/ldec_87s_5mpp.tif">GeoTIFF</a> of the number of LOLA returns in each pixel (count map
)
