New View of the Lunar South Pole from LOLA</h2><h4></h4><span style="margin-top: 2px">Michael Kenneth Barker</span><br><p><h3><u>Large-Area DEMs</u></h3>

<div><br></div>All TIF files are cloud-optimized GeoTiffs (<a href="https://www.cogeo.org/" target="_blank">COG</a>s) with south polar stereographic X/Y coordinates in meters and in the MOON_ME reference frame of the JPL DE421 ephemeris.<div><br></div><div>Filename format: PREFIX_LAT_PIX_STEMS.TIF</div><div><br></div><div>where PREFIX gives the data type (LDEM, LDEC, LDSM, LDRM, or LPSR), LAT gives the latitude limit (80S, 83S, 75S or&nbsp;</div><div>60S), PIX gives the pixel scale in meters per pixel (e.g., 10MPP, 20MPP, 30MPP, 40MPP, 60MPP, etc.), and STEMS&nbsp;</div><div>is an additional identifier (e.g., ADJ, ADJ_ERR, ADJ_HILL, ADJ_EFFRES, ADJ_ROUGH_100M, etc.).</div><div><br></div>

<div>For example, these are the filenames for the 20 meters/pixel DEM products which cover 80 - 90 deg South:</div><div><br></div>
<div>LDEC_80S_20MPP_ADJ.TIF - LOLA Digital Elevation Counts (LDEC, number of LOLA spots per pixel)</div>
<div>LDEM_80S_20MPP_ADJ.TIF - LOLA Digital Elevation Model (LDEM) giving surface height (in meters)</div>
<div>LDEM_80S_20MPP_ADJ_EFFRES.TIF - LDEM effective resolution (in meters)</div>
<div>LDEM_80S_20MPP_ADJ_ERR.TIF - LDEM surface height error (in meters)</div>
<div>LDEM_80S_20MPP_ADJ_HILL.TIF - LDEM hillshade (45 deg altitude, 45 deg azimuth)</div>
<div>LDSM_80S_20MPP_ADJ.TIF - LOLA Digital Slope Map (LDSM, in degrees)</div>
<div>LDSM_80S_20MPP_ADJ_ERR.TIF - LDSM slope error (in degrees)</div>
